Strength我的优势

My strength is connecting narrative, gameplay, and player experience into one clear design direction.我的优势在于把叙事、玩法与玩家体验整合成一个清晰的设计方向。

I focus on how mechanics create emotion, and how systems support the feeling I want players to have.我关注机制如何激发情绪，以及系统如何支撑我希望玩家获得的感受。

Developing Skill正在提升的能力

Tyrant's Court showed me how several systems at once can hide the decision a player is supposed to make. I want to get better at structuring a system so the core trade-off stays readable while the interaction still has depth.《朝堂之上》让我看到，多个系统同时出现时，玩家真正该做的决定会被盖住。我希望把系统结构做得更清楚，让核心取舍可读，同时互动仍然有深度。

Wonder Tales is still a facilitated paper prototype. The next technical step is a digital slice that a player can read without a host, so I can test whether the investigator role and card choices survive that change.Wonder Tales 目前仍是由主持人引导的纸面原型。下一步技术工作是做一个不需要主持人也能读懂的数字切片，用来检验调查者身份和卡牌选择在转换之后是否仍然成立。

I also want a tighter playtest method: write the question before the session, and separate what was designed, what players actually did, and what is only a future build.我也希望把试玩方法收得更紧：开测前先写下问题，并把已经设计的内容、玩家实际做过的事，以及只属于未来版本的功能分开。

Career Path职业方向

I aim to become a game designer focused on systems and level design.我的目标是成为一名专注于系统与关卡设计的游戏设计师。

I am especially interested in narrative-driven systems and player experience design, particularly in projects with strong emotional or worldbuilding elements.我尤其关注叙事驱动的系统设计与玩家体验设计，特别是情感表达强烈或世界观构建突出的项目。

6–12 Month Goal6–12 个月目标
